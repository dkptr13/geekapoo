Anime Boxes

Anime Boxes is a fully featured client for sites running Gelbooru, Danbooru and other similar sites, that allows you to browse among thousands of anime related images.

Features:

  • Configure your own list of servers
  • Tag Autocomplete
  • Favorites
  • Search history
  • Tag blacklist
  • Support for Videos, Animated Gifs and large images
  • Support for Pools and Notes
  • Support for Smartphones and Tablets
  • No Ads
  • And more!
